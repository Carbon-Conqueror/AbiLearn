Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AbiLearn is a free study website for CBSE Class 10 students. This page explains what data we collect and how it is used.

What we collect

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how the site is used. This collects:

  • Page views and visit counts
  • Country and device type (phone/desktop)
  • Browser type
  • Referring website (where you came from)

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites. No personal information (name, email, IP address) is stored or shared.

What we do not collect

  • We have no user accounts or registration
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party marketing pixels
  • We do not sell any data

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and provides analytics (cloudflare.com)
  • Google Fonts — provides the Plus Jakarta Sans typeface. Google may log font requests per their own privacy policy.

Cookies

AbiLearn does not set any cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless by design.

Children

AbiLearn is designed for students including those under 18. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone of any age.
