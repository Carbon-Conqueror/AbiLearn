AbiLearn is a CBSE Class 10 study platform designed to help students learn with clarity, practise with purpose, and walk into board exams with genuine confidence.

What We Are

AbiLearn is a structured study platform for CBSE Class 10. Every subject is organised chapter by chapter with notes, MCQ practice, past year board questions, and progress tracking — so students always know what to study next and how well they have covered it.

The platform covers all four core subjects: Mathematics, Science, English, and Social Science. Content is mapped directly to the NCERT syllabus and CBSE board exam patterns, with a focus on the specific chapters, question types, and marks-weightages that matter most in the actual exam.

Why It Exists

Most Class 10 students have the same textbooks, the same syllabus, and the same amount of time. What separates a 90+ score from an average one is rarely raw intelligence — it is a structured approach. Knowing what to study, when to revise, how to identify weak areas, and where to direct effort in the final weeks.

That structure used to be expensive, or scattered across dozens of sources. AbiLearn puts it in one place, completely free.

"Every student deserves a clear, honest map of their preparation — not just more content to consume."

How It Works

Open any subject. Pick a chapter. Read structured notes, attempt MCQs, and work through past year questions. Your progress is saved automatically. The dashboard shows which chapters are covered, where your weak spots are, and what to revise next. The Mistake Bank keeps a record of every question you got wrong, so nothing slips through permanently.

Study tips and exam strategies are available separately — not generic advice, but techniques specific to CBSE board preparation at the Class 10 level.
