Our Mission

"Every student deserves a system, not just a shortcut."

Most Class 10 students have access to the same textbooks, the same syllabus, and the same amount of time. What separates a 90+ score from an average one is a structured approach — knowing what to study, when to revise, how to identify weak spots, and where to focus in the final weeks. AbiLearn exists to give every student that system, completely free.

What AbiLearn Offers
📚

Chapter-wise Learning

Every subject broken down chapter by chapter with structured notes, key concepts, and NCERT-aligned explanations — so nothing important gets missed.

✅

MCQ Practice

Subject-specific multiple choice questions for active recall. Immediate feedback helps you understand not just what is wrong, but why.

📋

PYQs & Sample Papers

Past year board questions and sample papers organised by chapter and pattern — so you know exactly what CBSE has asked, and what it is likely to ask again.

📊

Progress Tracking

A Mistake Bank that logs every wrong answer, and a dashboard showing your readiness across subjects so revision always goes where it matters most.

💡

Tips & Strategies

Exam strategies, spaced repetition guides, subject-specific techniques, and an exam-day checklist — all the non-syllabus knowledge that actually moves scores.

🔍

Smart Search

Instant search across all chapters, topics, and formulas — so you can find exactly what you need without hunting through menus or textbook indexes.

Why AbiLearn Is Different
🎯

Purpose-built, not repurposed

AbiLearn is designed from the ground up for CBSE Class 10 — not a generic platform with a Class 10 filter applied. Every feature exists because Class 10 students need it.

⚡

Fast and distraction-free

No ads, no paywalls, no dark patterns. AbiLearn loads fast and gets out of the way so students can spend more time actually studying, not navigating the app.

🔄

A system, not a dump

Notes alone aren't enough. AbiLearn connects learning → practice → review → improvement in a single flow, ensuring students actually retain what they study.

🤝

Built with students, for students

Every feature on AbiLearn comes from real student pain points — the confusion of where to start, which topics to focus on, and how to know if preparation is actually working.
